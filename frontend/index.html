The Process

How Satyascan Determines Truth

Satyascan operates an autonomous multi-signal verification pipeline engineered to neutralize misinformation without human editorial latency.

1
Decomposition & Summarization

Complex articles are split into atomic verifiable assertions. Stopwords and conversational noise are removed to produce targeted multi-vector search queries.

2
Credibility-Weighted Synthesis

Evaluates domain authority against calculated stances to issue an unequivocal REAL, FAKE, or UNVERIFIED verdict with certainty scoring.
